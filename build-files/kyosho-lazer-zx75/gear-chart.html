Final drive ratio for the Lazer ZX-7.5, from page 36 of Kyosho's manual (No. 34331). Spur teeth run across and pinion teeth run down.

How it works. The car uses 48 pitch (48P) gears. Final drive ratio is spur teeth ÷ pinion teeth × 2.5, where 2.5 is the ratio built into this car. Every value on Kyosho's chart matches that formula. Blank cells (–) are combinations the manual does not list. The kit spur is 78T. The manual's spur parts are LA427-76 (76T), LA427-78 (78T, kit), and LA427-80 (80T); I did not see part numbers for the 82T, 72T, or 69T columns.

Choosing a starting ratio

  • Kyosho's motor suggestions for this car are 15.5T or 13.5T for 4WD, so a 17.5T is a milder motor than the manual assumes. That points toward a higher final drive ratio (a bigger spur or smaller pinion) than a hot motor would use on the same track.
  • On a small, tight track, start near the middle of the chart and move one tooth at a time, judging by motor temperature and the end of the main straight. These are not tested numbers; treat any specific ratio as a first guess.
  • If the motor or ESC is too hot to hold your hand on after a run, gear lower (higher ratio). If it runs cool and feels slow at the end of the straight, gear up (lower ratio).
  • Set the gear mesh with a sheet of paper between the gears, as the manual shows on page 29.

For how gearing works in general, see Tuning Lab: gearing.
