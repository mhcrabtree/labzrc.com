What else is worth tracking

  • Ride height and droop. Set these first; they change every other adjustment.
  • Sway bars. Stiffer front bar sharpens turn-in; stiffer rear bar adds rear stability on smooth tracks.
  • Weight balance and battery position. Moving the pack changes front/rear balance more than most small tweaks.
  • ESC and motor settings. Punch, drag brake, and timing change how the chassis loads as much as any shock change.
  • A setup log. Record track, temperature, tire, and lap times with every change.
