Tuning Lab: 1/10 Off-Road Buggies

What each adjustment does, which way to turn it, and where to start, with the front and rear of the car explained separately. These pages cover 2WD and 4WD buggies in the 17.5 stock class on dirt and clay. Carpet comes later.

Last edited: November 26, 2026

How to read these pages. Every number here is a starting point drawn from general tuning guidance and kit manuals, not a measured result from this garage. Change one thing at a time, change the front and rear separately, and trust your lap times over this site.

Putting it to use

The tuning pages are general. A build file records one specific car: its parts, its base setup, and what changed. See the build files.

What else is worth tracking

  • A setup log. Record track, temperature, tires, and lap times with every change.
  • ESC and motor settings. Punch, drag brake, and timing change how the chassis loads as much as any shock change.
  • Battery position. It moves weight front to rear faster than almost any other change.
