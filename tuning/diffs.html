Differentials

Diffs decide how power splits between wheels. A thicker diff oil acts more locked; a thinner oil lets the wheels turn more independently.

Last edited: November 25, 2026 · Dirt / clay, 17.5 stock class

From Kyosho's manual. Rows marked (manual) come from the Lazer ZX-7.5 instruction manual, No. 34331.

Starting points, not gospel. These numbers come from general tuning guidance, not measured results from this garage. Change one thing at a time.

The ZX-7.5 kit diffs

DiffKit oil (manual p. 12-13)Oil amount
Front (gear)#70001.7-1.8 g
Rear (gear)#50001.7-1.8 g
Center (gear)#2000001.4-1.5 g

Kyosho's manual also gives a grip scale for the front and rear diffs: #2000 for low grip, up to #10000 for high grip. Your high-bite clay sits toward the upper end of that scale, and the kit's #7000 front and #5000 rear are already on the high-grip half. Start with the kit oils and move one step at a time.

Kyosho sells these oils as #5000, #7000, and #200000 (40cc bottles). AMain also lists #40000 and #300000 if you want a step on the center. 1UP Racing also sells a wide range of pure silicone diff oil viscosities.

Ball diff vs gear diff

TypeProsCons
Ball diffSimple, adjustable by hand, no oil to leakWears, needs rebuilds, changes feel as it heats
Gear diff (oil)Consistent, tunable with oil weight, very smoothNeeds sealing; oil can leak; a bit more work to change

More racers are moving to gear diffs because the feel stays consistent through a race and tuning is a matter of swapping oil.

What each one does

Front diff

Thicker

  • More forward drive and stability
  • Less front rotation

Thinner

  • More turn-in, easier to steer
  • Less stability on power

Center diff

Thicker

  • More even front/rear drive split
  • Better on smooth, high-bite tracks

Thinner

  • More difference between front and rear speed
  • Can be calmer on low bite

Rear diff

Thicker

  • More straight-line bite
  • Less rotation off the throttle

Thinner

  • More rotation and rear steering
  • More wheel spin if too thin

Notes

  • Carpet runs thicker diffs; those numbers come later.
  • Change one diff at a time; moving all three at once hides what helped.
  • Always weigh or measure the oil quantity. The kit gives 1.7-1.8 g front and rear and 1.4-1.5 g center.
