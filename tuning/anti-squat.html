Anti-squat and kick-up

Pills, inserts, and holders change the angle of the arm pivots. At the rear that is anti-squat; at the front it is kick-up.

Last edited: November 26, 2026 · 1/10 off-road buggies, dirt and clay, 17.5 stock class

Starting points, not gospel. These effects are general tuning principles and the numbers are starting ranges, not measured results from this garage. Change one thing at a time, and change the front and rear separately.

Front kick-up and rear anti-squat

Pills, inserts, and holder positions change the angle of the arm pivots. At the rear that is anti-squat; at the front it is kick-up.

Each box shows what happens at that end of the car. Front and rear often do opposite things.

Front: kick-up

More kick-up

  • Steering: More on-power steering response; the front climbs into corners.
  • Traction: Better over bumps and ruts.
  • Stability: Less stable on the brakes.

Less kick-up

  • Steering: Calmer and steadier off-power.
  • Traction: The nose can dig in over rough sections.
  • Stability: More stable on the brakes and into corners.

Rear: anti-squat

More anti-squat

  • Steering: More on-power steering; the car turns better on throttle.
  • Traction: Less rear squat means less rear bite on throttle.
  • Stability: Flatter, more consistent jumps.

Less anti-squat

  • Steering: Less on-power steering; the car can push.
  • Traction: More squat and more rear hook-up under power.
  • Stability: Smoother, calmer power delivery.

The labels (degrees, letters, colors) differ from kit to kit, so record the setting and which way it moved the car.

Starting points

ConditionRear anti-squatFront kick-up
High-bite, smooth clay1-2 degreesLess kick-up for a calm front
Medium or low bite2-3 degreesMedium
Bumpy trackMediumMore kick-up to climb bumps

Pill and insert settings

Different cars label them differently: degrees, letters, or colored inserts. The effect is the same. Record the setting in your log and compare it with the kit manual, because the labels are not the same from car to car.
